Hi, I'm Yashwanth! 👋

I'm an AI and Full-Stack Engineer at LTM, interested in building practical AI systems, agentic workflows, and advanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) applications. My work spans Python backends, LLM-powered document retrieval, and full-stack products using React, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL. I've also worked on geospatial data pipelines and satellite-based environmental research during my time with ISRO.

Previously, I worked as an AI Research Intern with DLRL, where I built an offline AI question-answering system for secure document retrieval. At ISRO's National Remote Sensing Centre, I processed satellite observations and applied spatial statistics and long-term trend analysis to study regional air pollution. I enjoy solving problems where applied AI, data, and software engineering meet.

I graduated with a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from **[MVSR Engineering College](https://mvsrec.edu.in/)** in 2026. During college, I won four hackathon and project competitions, including HackSavvy, Encode, CSI Project Expo, and the MVSR project expo. I'm currently focused on AI engineering, backend development, RAG pipelines, and agentic systems.
