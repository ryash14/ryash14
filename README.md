Hi, I'm Yashwanth! 👋

I work on **AI engineering** at <img src="assets/profile-readme/org-ltm.svg" width="32" align="texttop" alt="" /> [LTM](https://www.ltm.com), building **agentic workflows and retrieval systems** with LangGraph, MCP and vector search. I like owning the whole path from idea to product: the model and retrieval layer, the backend, and the interface people actually use.

Previously, I was an Applied AI Intern at <img src="assets/profile-readme/org-drdo.svg" width="38" align="texttop" alt="" /> [DRDO (DLRL)](https://www.drdo.gov.in), where I built an **air-gapped retrieval system** that runs with no external APIs, no cloud inference and no internet at all: local models, embeddings, vector search and reranking, entirely on-premise. Before that, I was a Research Intern at <img src="assets/profile-readme/org-isro.svg" width="36" align="texttop" alt="" /> [ISRO (NRSC)](https://www.nrsc.gov.in), reconstructing missing satellite observations with DINEOF (R² = 0.78) and analysing atmospheric pollution across India and the Indo-Pacific using OMI and Sentinel-5P data.

I led the web development team at <img src="assets/profile-readme/org-mvsrec.svg" width="54" align="texttop" alt="" /> [MVSREC](https://mvsrec.edu.in) and won 4 hackathons, including **1st at HackSavvy 2025** (130+ teams) and **2nd at Encode 2025** (85 teams).
